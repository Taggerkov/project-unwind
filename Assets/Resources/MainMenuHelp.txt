<size=32><b>About Project Unwind</b></size>

Project Unwind is a 2.5D fighter made in Unity 6.3. It is highly inspired by Arc System Works games yet aims to be its own. Unfortunately this is a very early build and the main unique features are missing or a work in progress. This is barely a prototype.

<size=27><b>How to Play</b></size>

Best of 3 rounds, 99 seconds each. Drain your opponent to zero health, or be ahead on health when time runs out, to take the round.

<size=27><b>Inputs</b></size>

Listed as Keyboard / Controller. Forward means toward the opponent.
Move: W A S D / left stick or D-pad
Light: U / West (X, Square)
Medium: I / North (Y, Triangle)
Heavy: O / East (B, Circle)
Unique: L / South (A, Cross)
Ability: K / right trigger
Guard: J / left trigger
Menus: directions to move, Unique (L / South) to confirm.

<size=27><b>Concepts</b></size>

- Directions use numpad notation, always relative to the opponent (6 toward, 4 away).
- Combat runs on a fixed 60 Hz tick for deterministic timing.
- Moves flow through Startup, Active and Recovery, with Hitstun and Blockstun on contact.
- Moves can cancel into others: neutral, kara, IASA, gatling (on hit or block) and whiff routes.
- The current fighter, the Redeemer, has walks, crouch, jumps, a forward run, dashes, a normal and a three hit Rekka chain.

<size=27><b>Limitations</b></size>

- Only one character is playable, so matches are mirror only.
- Signature systems (Stamina and Unique Abilities) are not implemented yet.
- Stages, audio and UI are placeholder or WIP.
- Expect missing animations, rough edges and debug values.
